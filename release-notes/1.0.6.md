## What's new in R1 1.0.6

### New

- Route optimization: press PLAN ROUTE to lock an optimised route of pickups and drop-offs. It only changes when you accept new contracts or press RE-PLAN.
- New contracts accepted after planning are added to the remaining route at the best spot, and the route shows where they went.
- Dropped or abandoned contracts stay in the route greyed out, so you can see what happened.
- The overlay shows the next stops and can plan the route in game.

### Changed

- With Route optimization on, the route is a preview until you press PLAN ROUTE; the planned route is saved and kept after a restart.
