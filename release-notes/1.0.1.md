## What's new in R1 1.0.1

### New
- **Problem stops stand out.** A stop whose place wasn't found is shown in red and one the app is unsure about in
  amber: on the Jobs, Next and Learned Data tabs the whole row is tinted, and a collapsed job says how many of its
  steps need a place or a check. On the overlay only the words are tinted, including the
  "Next Location" place, and the SCAN CONTRACT confirm screen tints the "→ place" words. Click the tag to fix the place.

### Fixed
- **Overlay stays on the stop you chose.** With "Return to first stop" off, the card now stays on the stop you moved
  to with the arrows (it only moves when that stop is completed or abandoned). Before, it kept jumping back to the
  first stop. With the setting on, it goes back to the first stop after the set time, or straight away when your
  start really changes. Reading your position and saving jobs no longer move the card.
- **Overlay follows the work mode.** Switching Work mode to Other (or back to Haul) while the app runs now switches the overlay too. Before, the overlay kept showing the Haul card until another overlay setting was changed. Other-mode settings the overlay uses (distances on the overlay, start mode, distance from mission markers, show reputation) also reach it straight away.
- **Overlay buttons no longer overlap when it's narrow.** GO HERE, ABANDON and AFK keep their size and spacing; when
  the overlay is narrow the "SUPERCARGO · OTHER MODE" label moves to its own line, and AFK moves down instead of
  sliding under ABANDON.

- **Fix panel can't save to the wrong stop.** If the overlay moves to another stop while a place fix is open, the fix
  closes instead of applying to the new stop.

### Changed
- **Start line moved to the bottom of the overlay.** The FROM row (start, accuracy, match tag and the 📍 WHERE AM I pin)
  now sits under the buttons with a gap, and the pin is easier to hit, so a click meant for the pin no longer lands on
  a button.
- **WHERE AM I names the station.** At a station the start now shows the station's name (for example "Seraphim
  Station", or "Seraphim Station (hangar)" in a hangar) instead of "Open space". Outposts and landing zones show their
  name and planet; "Open space" is used only when you are not at a station, a place or near a planet.
