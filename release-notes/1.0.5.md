## What's new in R1 1.0.5

### New

- Settings → Other Mode → Distances & Route: Route optimization (off by default) plans a shorter route for your stops while keeping each pickup before its delivery.
- With Route optimization on, the Next tab shows how much distance the planned route saves, and the next stop only changes mid-trip when the new route is clearly shorter.

### Changed

- From Start no longer lists a delivery above its own pickup.
